Hypothesis: useful complete surfaces reach judgment and teaching within four searches when distractor operations span many resources, not only one repeated entity. This freezes coverage, not a production candidate. Keep v13, contracts, one logical judgment and 16,000-byte packets fixed. No provider calls, dependencies or full agent evaluation.

Four structural cases reuse the consumed abstract laboratory: service effect, membership, independent outcomes with an opaque routine, and same-resource read/action composition. Labels and requests are explicit in cases.json. These are development controls, not fresh domains or a holdout. Two profiles, two catalogue sizes (0/100 added operations), repeated/two-or-three-request cycling and forward/reverse insertion produce 32 measured variants. Zero-growth profile duplicates are intentional controls, not independent evidence.

Profiles cycle four distinct authored archive purposes. Concentrated assigns all added operations to one opaque entity; distributed assigns each to a separate opaque entity. Each entity copies Receipt's id-field shape without relations. Capabilities use side-effect outputs; only owning entity changes between paired profiles. Copy the canonical record capability, replace its purpose, preserve the common historical-only side effect, and assign opaque operation/resource IDs. This varies resource ownership and purpose diversity; it does not yet cover heterogeneous field shapes or independently authored domains.

All added archive operations are unrelated under the fixed labels. Useful labels never enter ranking or allocation. No gold path is supplied to production code. Outcomes require complete labelled capability sets and actual native relation visibility where specified; uncertain routine retention is mandatory. The horizon remains 24 and the quality gate four searches.
