The case called `large_path` varies the target contract's length; it does **not**
add a large required acquisition. Its measured standalone size exceeds the
budget. Repeated prerequisite evidence and a genuinely feasible allocation loss
remain coverage gaps. Preserve this result rather than silently recasting it as
an allocator defect or adding a task-shaped boost.

Old starvation-v2.1 supplies alternative-supplier, cross-catalogue and distractor
growth coverage; it is not removed or superseded. New coverage can be appended
under a new frozen version before design changes. There is no sealed acceptance
score and no end-to-end success prediction from these development controls.
