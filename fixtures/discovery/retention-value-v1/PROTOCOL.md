# Operational ablation: value of rejecting admitted candidates

Hypothesis: semantic rejection may reduce declaration growth, but its interaction
with additive exposure/frontier state may also affect calls to complete useful
paths. Measure both; classification accuracy is not the endpoint.

Single factor: fixture judgment versus retaining every admitted target as uncertain.
The latter is a diagnostic counterfactual, not a production bypass or a semantic
claim. Production ranking, root order, allocation, full contracts, graph rules,
16,000 UTF-8 byte/search admission, one logical fixture response/search and 24-search
horizon stay fixed. No provider, embeddings, effects, dependencies or AppWorld run.
Because packet construction is preserved, this does not measure an implementation
that removes packet construction or estimate actual token/cost savings.

Use all frozen development cohorts unchanged: trajectory-v4 (18), resource-breadth-v5
(32), root-diversity-v1 ordinary controls (8). These are 58 variants sharing a schema,
not independent domains or a sealed holdout. Verify their existing locks before and
after. Labels remain at the fixture judgment/scoring boundary, never in selection.
Freeze this protocol and harness source before measurement. One pass per arm with
forward/reverse insertion parity. Alternate arm order by variant index.

The diagnostic harness must teach every retained catalogue, including distractors,
in the same monotonic session. Record complete exposed references and actual full
declaration bytes. Preserve the original relation visibility and outcome requirements.
Assert authorization, complete contracts, fixed admission limits, insertion parity
and append-only exposure. Preserve baseline semantics: compare first completion,
prefix request/byte cost and taught declaration size with surviving baseline evidence.
If parity fails, stop interpretation and retain the harness fault; do not tune cases.

Primary measures per variant: first complete useful path, per-outcome completion,
four-search availability, missing-outcome area, no-gain searches, prefix logical
requests/admitted bytes, declaration bytes, incidental exposure and repeated rejection.
Report post-completion work separately. Record indexing, selection and teaching CPU
time separately from unavailable provider latency/tokens/cost. Never assign invented
completion times to incomplete runs.

Report original fixture quality criteria separately: the original no-unrelated-
exposure condition is expected to fail for retain-admitted and is not relabelled.
Operational dominance requires no later completion/request cost in any previously
completed variant, at least one starvation recovery, no lost opaque/independent/
membership outcome, and no declaration-size increase. Mixed results are a tradeoff,
not adoption. A null result still locates where work should move. No production
adoption follows this diagnostic, even on dominance; caller burden and execution
are unmeasured. Do not select a retention quota or threshold after seeing results.

Stop after both arms/all cohorts. Abort on harness integrity failure, preserve partial
records, no silent rerun or second arm revision. No provider or full agent evaluation.
The next intervention must follow operational evidence across caller, retrieval,
allocation, judgment, frontier and exposure rather than a classifier-only objective.
