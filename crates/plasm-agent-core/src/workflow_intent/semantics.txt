Requirement graph semantics (shared contract):
effect is a requested action or output. information_need is evidence to obtain. selection_constraint qualifies affirmative operations through constrains links. condition is a predicate with when_true or when_false links to affirmative operations. before orders affirmative operations; array position does not. Effect and information_need are the only operation kinds.
A prohibition is an independent restriction with its own scope. It has no relationship endpoints; never require a constrains or branch link for it. Selection constraints on neighboring operations do not narrow a prohibition.
Read the effective graph compositionally: an operation together with its attached constraints, branch predicates and ordering defines its meaning. An operation need not duplicate those statements. Requirement-owned decisions preserve unresolved meaning and plausible alternatives. They are not selected branches. Affected requirements and their downstream operations remain provisional; capability availability does not settle them. Retirement records are history, not current requirements. This is a current workflow interpretation, not permanent policy or proof of execution.
Classify restrictions by their meaning, not their grammatical presentation: an instruction forbidding an action is a prohibition even when conditional. It is never an affirmative effect to perform. An unresolved decision may vary only the unsettled portion of a requirement; explicit restrictions remain in force under every alternative.
